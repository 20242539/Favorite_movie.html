Furious 7

Introduction

Deckard Shaw seeks revenge against Dominic Toretto and his family for his comatose brother

Plot

Aftew defeating international terrorist Owen Shaw, Dominic Toretto, Brian O'Conner and the rest of the crew have separated to return to more normal lives. However, Deckard Shaw, Owen's older brother, is thirsty for revenge. A slick government agent offers to help Dom and company take care of Shaw in exchange for their help in rescuing a kidnapped computer hacker who has developed a powerful surveillance program.

Cast

Vin Diesel as Dominic Toretto

A former criminal, professional street racer and millionaire who has retired and settled down with his wife, Letty

Paul Walker as Brian O’Conner

A former police officer and FBI agent turned criminal who has retired and settled down with his wife and their son, Jack.

Michelle Rodriguez as Letty Ortiz

Doms wife and a professional street racer

Jason Statham as Decker Shaw

A former special forces soldier and intelligence agent who seeks to avenge his comatose younger brother after his hospitalization at the hands of Dom and his team in Spain.
